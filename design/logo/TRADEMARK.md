# CAELIS — trademark check (preliminary, not legal advice)

## What was actually checked

- Two general web searches for "Caelis" together with astronomy / camera / intervalometer, and for "CAELIS" with EUIPO / USPTO / TMview and class 9.
- Result: nothing relevant found. **This is not evidence that the name or logo is free.** Web search does not query the trademark registers, and the registers (EUIPO eSearch plus, TMview, WIPO Global Brand Database, USPTO, Czech ÚPV) could not be searched from here.
- No logo / image similarity search was made at all.

So: availability of the name **CAELIS** and of the **symbol** is unknown.

## What to do before a public release

1. **Word mark search** in these registers, in at least Czech Republic (ÚPV), EU (EUIPO) and any market where the app will be published (USPTO for US):
   - EUIPO eSearch plus / TMview (covers EU and Czech national marks in one search)
   - WIPO Global Brand Database (international registrations)
   - USPTO trademark search
2. **Search terms:** CAELIS, plus phonetic and visual neighbours: CAELUS, CELIS, CELIES, KAELIS, COELIS, CAELIX, CAELIS-prefixed names. Latin *caelum* (sky) means other businesses may use similar names.
3. **Classes:** 9 (downloadable software / apps), 42 (software as a service), 41 (education / entertainment if the app grows into it), and 12 / 35 only if you sell hardware or retail. Describe the goods precisely: "downloadable mobile application for time-lapse photography and astronomical observation".
4. **Figurative search** for the symbol: Vienna classification 01.01 (stars, comets) in combination with the viewfinder / dial elements; reverse image search (Google Lens, TinEye) on `caelis-icon.svg` rendered as PNG.
5. **Know the crowded areas:** star and "sparkle" shapes are very common app and AI-product icons, and camera viewfinder corners are common in photo apps. Distinctiveness comes from the combination (eight-point star with long and short tips + viewfinder + twelve-tick dial) and from the word mark, not from any one element.
6. **Domain, store and handle checks:** Google Play and App Store name search, domain `caelis.*`, GitHub / social handles. A free trademark register does not mean a free store name.
7. If the app is to be published commercially, let an IP attorney run a full clearance search and file; costs are usually small compared with a forced rename.

## Open-source note

The repository is GPL-3.0. That licence covers the code, not the name or logo. If you want others not to ship forks under the CAELIS name and logo, state that in the README (for example "name and logo are not covered by GPL-3.0; do not use them for derived products") and consider registering the word mark.
